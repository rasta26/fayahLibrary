# Template: Mentoring 1:1 prep

## 1. Purpose
An agenda and talking points for a weekly 1:1, grounded in evidence from the mentee's recent work.
For me; private unless I choose to share it.

## 2. Inputs
- Mentee and level (required)
- Last 1:1's action items (ask if missing)
- Since then: their PRs and my review growth notes (if none, say the prep rests only on what I gave)
- Their goals, and anything they asked to discuss (optional)
- Meeting length (default 30 minutes)

## 3. Steps
1. Mark each of last time's action items done or open, with evidence.
2. Pull patterns from the growth notes: strengths with evidence, recurring gaps with a count.
3. Pick one growth focus only.
4. Match it to a practice: a kata (see `kata.md`) or one reading.
5. Put their topics first, then wins, then the growth focus. Split the time.
6. Draft 2–3 open questions.
7. Propose 1–3 action items, each with an owner and a date.

## 4. Output format
```
# 1:1 prep: <mentee> (<date>, <n> min)
## Last time's action items
- [done|open] <item>: <evidence>
## Their topics (<n> min)
## Wins to name (<n> min)
- <win>: <PR link or note>
## One growth focus (<n> min)
Pattern: <gap> (seen <n> times: <links>)
Practice: <kata or reading>
## Questions to ask
## Proposed action items
- <item> (owner, due date)
```
One page at most. Tone: supportive and specific.

## 6. Done check
- [ ] Every win and gap cites evidence (a link or a note).
- [ ] There is exactly one growth focus.
- [ ] Their topics come first.
- [ ] Every action item has an owner and a date.
- [ ] It fits on one page.
